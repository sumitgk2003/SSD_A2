Stoplight Game (Nash Equilibrium)

In the Stoplight Game, two players (You and Computer) each choose to either Go or Stop at a stoplight. If both Go, they crash (worst outcome). If both Stop, they wait (not optimal). If one Goes and the other Stops, the Goer gets ahead. The Nash Equilibrium is for one to Go and the other to Stop.

Payoff Table:
Computer: GoComputer: Stop
You: Go-10, -10+1, 0
You: Stop0, +10, 0
